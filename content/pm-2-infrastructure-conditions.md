# Bridge Conditions

Every structure that is at least 20 feet long with vehicles traveling over or under it is subject to National Bridge Inspection Standards [(NBIS)](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/bridge/nbis.cfm) and must be inspected at least every two years. Bridges are rated on a scale from 1-9 on various bridge elements, including the deck, substructure, superstructure, culvert walls and floor, and/or retaining walls. To be considered in "good" condition, each bridge element must be rated a "7" or higher. Structures with any element receiving a rating of "4" or lower receives a "poor" rating.

## Pavement Conditions
It is important to make sure that our existing network of roads are maintained from the basic wear and tear of passenger and truck traffic. Starting in 2018, every state Department of Transportation will start collecting data on the roughness, rutting, faulting, and cracking of pavement on all Interstate Highways, with data available in 2019. Non-Interstate roads on the National Highway System will begin collecting data in 2020, with data available beginning in 2020. For now, pavement conditions are measured by the "roughness" of the pavement, as measured by the International Roughness Index.
