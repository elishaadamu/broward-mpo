# Plots

## By approving these targets, TCAMPO commits to:
* Working with VDOT and partners to address pavement and bridge condition needs;
* Incorporating infrastructure condition measures and targets into the LRTP;
* Aligning transportation planning efforts with applicable state infrastructure goals; and
* Linking MTIP investments to progress toward achieving pavement and bridge condition targets.
