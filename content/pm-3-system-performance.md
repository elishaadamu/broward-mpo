# System Performance

## Are we building a highway system that can efficiently move people and goods?

Per federal law, the Broward MPO on November 14, 2018 initially adopted System Performance targets and readopted those targets on December 8, 2022. These targets will help to assess travel time reliability on the Interstate and non-Interstate National Highway System and help to assess freight movement on the Interstate system.
