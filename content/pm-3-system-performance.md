# Plots

## By approving these targets, TCAMPO commits to:
* Collaborating with VDOT and regional stakeholders to address travel reliability, freight mobility, congestion, and emissions.
* Incorporating these performance, freight, and emissions targets into the Long-Range Transportation Plan (LRTP).
* Coordinating metropolitan planning efforts with Virginia's statewide goals and performance measures.
* Linking planned investments in the Metropolitan Transportation Improvement Program (MTIP) directly to achieving adopted performance targets.
