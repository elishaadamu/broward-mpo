# What is the Broward Metropolitan Planning Organization?

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/mindmixerprod/image/upload/w_1400,c_limit,f_auto/20171130214355-db6f33b2.png)

A [Metropolitan Planning Organization (MPO)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_planning_organization) is an organization that is tasked by the federal government with making policy on local transportation issues and deciding how to spend federal transportation funds within the region. The Broward MPO plays this role for Broward County. The MPO seeks to address overall mobility needs within the County, based on the needs and aspirations of residents, businesses, and visitors. The mission of the MPO is to collaboratively plan, prioritize, and fund the delivery of diverse transportation options.

## Broward MPO Vision Statement

> *Our work will have* **measurable positive impact by ensuring transportation projects are well selected, funded and delivered.**

The Broward MPO Performance Measures dashboard aims to assist planners and policymakers in tracking federally-mandated performance measures (which include Safety, Infrastructure, and System Performance) in order to fulfill the Broward MPO vision of having measurable impact.

## What is Transportation Performance Management?

Transportation Performance Management (TPM) is an effort to measure and improve the performance of our regional transportation systems. Federal law (the Moving Ahead for Progress in the 21st Century Act [MAP-21] and the Fix America's Surface Transportation Act [FAST Act]) has mandated the implementation of various performance metrics, so that State Departments of Transportation (DOTs) and Metropolitan Planning Organizations (MPOs) make smarter transportation investments and better policy decisions. Every MPO and State DOT is in the early stage of measuring and analyzing initial performance data. As we learn more about the conditions of our infrastructure, the safety of our system, and its overall performance, we will be able to adjust our infrastructure investments and policies to achieve better outcomes for everyone.

## How is the Broward MPO Governed?

The Broward MPO board has 25 voting members, including representatives from many municipalities, the Broward County Commission, the Broward County School Board, and the South Florida Regional Transportation Authority ("SFRTA"/"Tri-Rail"). [Learn more about our board here](https://browardmpo.org/our-board).
