## By approving these targets, TCAMPO commits to:

- Coordinating with VDOT and safety partners to reduce traffic fatalities and serious injuries;
- Incorporating the Commonwealth's safety performance measures and targets into the Long-Range Transportation Plan (LRTP);
- Integrating the goals and objectives of the Virginia Strategic Highway Safety Plan (SHSP) into the metropolitan transportation planning process; and
- Demonstrating in the Metropolitan Transportation Improvement Program (MTIP) how planned investments support the achievement of the adopted safety targets.
