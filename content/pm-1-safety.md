## PM-1

# Safety

## Safer streets are vital to a functional transportation system.

Safety is rightfully the first transportation performance measure. Everyone should have the right to get to their destination unharmed, regardless of whether they are driving, riding public transit, bicycling, or walking. As distracted driving, alcohol, and excessive speed make streets more dangerous, MPOs, State DOTs, and local governments must work for safer outcomes through better design, education, and law enforcement. Broward MPO and the State of Florida have set a goal of zero for fatal and serious injury crashes.


## 65% of all fatal and serious injury crashes occur on 14.4% of streets.
Just 721 centerline miles of the 4,984-mile street network in Broward recorded an average of 1,384 serious injury or fatal crashes from 2019-23.

## Total Fatalities are decreasing.
The five-year average for fatalities had steadily increased since the 2010-2014 time period. However, the total annual fatalities decreased in both 2022 and 2023 thereby causing a decrease in the five-year average for fatalities for the 2019-2023 time period.

## The rate of fatal crashes is decreasing.
Because it is typical for an area to see an increase in fatalities as the amount of driving increases, we also measure the crash rate per 100 million vehicle miles traveled (VMT). The crash rate per 100 million VMT for 2019-2023 was lower than 2018-2022, the first decrease since the 2010-2014 time period.

## Non-Motorized Fatalities and Serious Injuries have been slightly decreasing.
Non-motorized (that is, bicycle and pedestrian) fatalities and injuries have been decreasing steadily since 2013. Although Broward did experience spikes in 2016 and 2021, the overall trend continues downward. There are a number of initiatives being planned and implemented to ensure the trend continues downward.
